APPLICATIONS

Industries We Serve

Our polymers are used across key industries — from renewable energy to automotive and food packaging

Photovoltaics (Solar Energy)

EVA and POE encapsulants for protecting photovoltaic cells. They provide sealing, optical clarity, and module durability for 25+ years.

EVA POE POP

Automotive

Seals, bumpers, fuel lines, hoses, and interior components. EPDM, PA12, and POE are key materials for reducing vehicle weight and improving reliability.

EPDM PA12 POE OBC

Flexible Packaging

Barrier multilayer films for long-shelf-life food products. EVOH provides the oxygen barrier, while EAA serves as the adhesive tie layer.

EVOH EAA EMAA MLLDPE

Cables and Wires

Insulation for high-voltage power cables, telecommunications cables, and renewable-energy cables. XLPE provides heat resistance and mechanical strength.

XLPE MLLDPE EAA

Construction

Roofing membranes, waterproofing, profiles, and pipes for water supply and heating. EPDM and XLPE are the standard for long-lasting building materials.

EPDM XLPE POE

Footwear Industry

Outsoles, midsoles, and insoles made from EVA, OBC, and POE. Lightness, flexibility, cushioning, and wear resistance are the key material requirements.

EVA OBC POE

Medical and Pharmaceutical

Flexible packaging for medical devices, IV bags, and surgical gloves. Compliant with FDA and USP Class VI requirements.

EVOH MLLDPE PA12

Electronics and Optics

Electronic housings, LED lenses, optical discs, and connectors. PC and PC-ABS are indispensable materials for high-precision molding.

PC PA66

Oil, Gas and Petrochemicals

Pipelines, hoses, and seals for oil wells and trunk pipelines. The chemical resistance of EPDM and PA12 makes them the optimal choice for aggressive environments.

EPDM PA12 XLPE
