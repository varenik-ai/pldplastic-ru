LCP
Liquid Crystal Polymers

LCP (Liquid Crystal Polymer) is a high-performance thermoplastic with extremely low warpage, outstanding dimensional stability, excellent high-frequency electrical properties and high HDT.

Product name: LCP (Liquid Crystal Polymer)
Request a Quote All Products

Description

LCP self-reinforces during processing (melt orientation of rigid-rod molecules), providing exceptional stiffness and strength with minimal warpage. Excellent high-frequency dielectric properties, chemical resistance and flame retardancy (UL94 V-0). Used in miniaturised precision components.

Applications

LCP is used in 5G/millimetre-wave connectors, miniature SMT components, dental instruments, optical reflectors and high-frequency PCBs.
