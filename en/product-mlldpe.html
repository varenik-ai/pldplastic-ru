MLLDPE
Metallocene Polyethylene

MLLDPE (Metallocene Linear Low-Density Polyethylene) is produced using single-site metallocene catalysts, giving a narrow molecular weight distribution and uniform comonomer distribution.

Product name: MLLDPE (Metallocene Linear Low Density Polyethylene)
Request a Quote All Products

Description

MLLDPE offers superior mechanical properties vs. conventional LLDPE: higher tensile strength, better puncture resistance, lower heat-seal initiation temperature and excellent clarity. Suitable for downgauging (thinner films at same performance).

Applications

MLLDPE is used in stretch films, heavy-duty bags, agricultural films, food packaging, hygienic films and industrial liners.
