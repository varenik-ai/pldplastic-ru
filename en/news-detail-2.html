What Is PBT

PBT plastic is a material based on polybutylene terephthalate. Polybutylene terephthalate (also known as polytetramethylene terephthalate, abbreviated PBT) is a polymer produced by the polycondensation of terephthalic acid and 1,4-butanediol. PBT and PET belong to the same class of materials — thermoplastic polyesters.
